About LUMU

"LUMU" (Lujie Wang × Zhicheng Mu) is the most talked-about duo in the T-Family fandom right now. Recently, they've drawn attention through their duo card from the 2026 TF Family Summer Sports Games, fan support events, and a steady stream of fan content.

Wang Lujie

Wang Lujie is a Mainland Chinese singer and actor, a trainee under Beijing Time Peak Culture & Art Development Co., Ltd.'s TF Family. Since October 6, 2022, he has appeared in original variety shows and various performances as a 4th-generation TF Family trainee.

LUMU Duo

LUMU is a phenomenon-level, top-tier Chinese duo idol group formed by Wang Lujie and Mu Zhicheng. Their distinctive musical style and stage presence have earned them enormous popularity within the TF Family and the wider fan community.

Mu Zhicheng

Mu Zhicheng is a Mainland Chinese singer and actor. In August 2016, he was selected into the TF Family training camp. Since October 2019, he has taken part in TF Family 3rd-generation trainee group variety shows and performances, launching his entertainment career.
